THE CHARCOAL SHAWARMA — WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: the complete responsive website
- images/: your four supplied images, used by the website

How to view:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or another browser.

How to publish:
Upload index.html and the complete images folder together to your hosting provider (for example, GitHub Pages, Netlify, or your web server). Keep the image filenames and folder structure unchanged.

Before launch:
- Add your real address, opening hours, phone/WhatsApp number, and social links.
- Replace the menu descriptions with your final menu and prices.
- Confirm any food preparation claims reflect your actual operations.
